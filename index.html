3D mode: each target is located by its horizontal departure (HD) along an azimuth (0° = North, 90° = East) and is drawn as a horizontal disc whose radius equals the tolerance. At spud, the reference azimuth is aligned with Target 1 so Build kicks off toward Target 1, while Left / Right / Custom Toolface steer in full 3D.
Offset well: surface slot 150 ft NE (Az 045°), kicks off at 2,500 ft and builds 2.0°/90 ft to 45°.
